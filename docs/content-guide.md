# Managing your portfolio

Everything on the site — your work, your biography, the homepage — is managed in the admin at **your-site.com/admin**. Sign in with the email and password you were given.

Changes go live as soon as you press **Publish**. There is nothing else to do: no code, no waiting for a developer.

---

## The admin at a glance

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Artworks** | Single works, each with its own page |
| **Projects** | Work with a story: a commission, a film, a series — brief, process images, films, credits |
| **Disciplines** | Painting, Illustration, Animation… the areas of work in the menu, and how each page is laid out |
| **Media** | Every image, clip and PDF you've uploaded |
| **Site settings** | The homepage picks, the availability note, contact wording and topics |
| **Profile** | Your name, biographies, portrait, email and social links |
| **Users** | People who can sign in |

## Publish, draft and preview

Artworks, projects and disciplines have three buttons at the top:

- **Publish changes** — puts the page live, and updates everywhere it appears (homepage, work list, discipline page).
- **Save draft** — keeps your changes without showing them to visitors.
- **Preview** (the square-with-arrow icon) — opens the site showing your draft, with a dark bar at the top. Press **Exit preview** in the bar when you're done.

To take something off the site without deleting it, open the **⋮** menu and choose **Unpublish**.

Every save is kept under **Versions**, so you can look back at, or restore, an earlier version.

## Add an artwork

1. **Artworks → Create new.**
2. **Title.**
3. **Image** — drag in the file or choose one you've already uploaded. Use a large original (2000–3000 px on the long edge); the site makes small, fast versions automatically. The work is never cropped.
4. **Discipline** — e.g. Painting.
5. Optional: series, year, medium, dimensions, a few words about the work, the project it belongs to, a credit line.
6. **Publish changes.**

The web address (e.g. `/artwork/blue-hour`) is filled in from the title. If you change it later, the old address keeps working.

**Featured** (on the right) moves a work to the front of lists.

**Order:** in the Artworks list, drag the ⋮⋮ handle to change the order works appear in.

## Add a project

**Projects → Create new.** The editor has tabs:

- **Overview** — summary, disciplines (the first is the main label), cover image, and what sits at the top of the page: the cover, a different image, or a film.
- **Story** — the brief, your approach, and **Sections**: blocks of text, images (stacked, in pairs, or a small sheet for process work) or films, in any order. Drag sections to reorder them.
- **Details** — year, client, your role, deliverables, collaborators, credits, related projects.
- **Search and sharing** — optional; leave empty to use the title and summary.

**Films:** paste the normal YouTube or Vimeo link from your browser's address bar, and choose a still image to show before it plays. For a very short clip you can upload an MP4 instead.

Artworks that belong to a project (set **Part of project** on the artwork) appear on the project page.

## Images and descriptions

Every image needs a **description** (alt text). It is read aloud to people who can't see the image, and helps search engines.

- Say what is visible: subject, composition, colour, mood. One or two sentences.
- Don't start with "Image of" or repeat the title.
- Good: *"A woman in a red coat crosses a rain-soaked street at dusk, her reflection breaking in the puddles."*
- Purely decorative images (textures, backgrounds) can tick **Decorative** instead.

**Focal point:** some layouts crop images to a fixed shape — the discipline tiles on the homepage, and film stills. Open the image in **Media**, choose **Edit image**, and click the most important part to keep it in frame.

To change an image's description everywhere it's used, edit it once in **Media**.

## Disciplines

Each discipline has a page at `/work/<name>` and a **page layout**:

| Layout | Looks like | Suits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gallery | Large works hung one by one, like an exhibition | Painting |
| Grid | Denser rows grouped by series | Illustration |
| Screening | Showreel and films on a dark background | Animation |
| Index | A typographic list of projects | Anything else |

To **rename** the fourth discipline ("Studio Projects"), just change its name — and its web address if you like; old links keep working. Drag disciplines in the list to change their order in the menu. Unpublishing a discipline hides it and all its work.

## The homepage — Site settings

- **Artwork beside your name** — the large image at the top.
- **Short silent loop** — optional few-second MP4 shown instead of that image.
- **Availability note** — the short line above your name, e.g. "Booking commissions for spring". It opens an email to you. Leave it empty to hide it.
- **Selected work** — your strongest 4–8 pieces, in order. Drag to reorder.
- **Featured project** — shown large on a dark background.

The **Contact** tab sets the invitation at the end of each page ("Have a project in mind?") and the topics on the contact page — each opens an email with that subject.

## About you — Profile

Name, headline, short and full biography, statement, how you work, portrait, studio images, email, location (only if you want it public), social links, a CV (PDF), experience and recognition.

All contact buttons open the visitor's own email app or Gmail, addressed to the **Email** here. There is no contact form.

**Recognition** (clients, exhibitions, publications, awards) is only shown when filled in. Only list what you're happy to make public.

## Writing text

Long text fields (biography, descriptions, brief, approach) keep it simple: **leave a blank line between paragraphs**.

## Adding someone else

**Users → Create new**, with their email and a password. Anyone with a login can edit everything, so only add people you trust.

## If something looks wrong

- **A change doesn't show:** check you pressed **Publish changes**, not Save draft. Then reload the page once.
- **An image looks blurry:** upload a larger file.
- **A page is missing:** check it's published, and that its discipline is published.
